The linear family

AR, MA and ARMA

Autoregressive, AR(ppp)

xt=∑k=1pφk xt−k+εt.x_t=\sum_{k=1}^{p}\varphi_k\,x_{t-k}+\varepsilon_t .xt​=k=1∑p​φk​xt−k​+εt​.

The value is a linear combination of its own past plus a fresh kick. It is the discrete version of a linear differential equation of order ppp driven by noise: AR(1) is relaxation, AR(2) can be a damped oscillator. Stationary only if the roots of 1−∑φkz−k1-\sum\varphi_kz^{-k}1−∑φk​z−k lie inside the unit circle — the discrete analogue of requiring the poles to be in the stable half-plane.

Moving average, MA(qqq)

xt=εt+∑k=1qϑk εt−k.x_t=\varepsilon_t+\sum_{k=1}^{q}\vartheta_k\,\varepsilon_{t-k}.xt​=εt​+k=1∑q​ϑk​εt−k​.

A finite impulse response: memory of exactly qqq steps, so ρ(k)=0\rho(k)=0ρ(k)=0 for k>qk>qk>q — a sharp cut-off in the correlation function that AR models never show.

ARMA(p,qp,qp,q) and ARIMA(p,d,qp,d,qp,d,q)

Both together; "I" means difference the series ddd times first, to remove a trend or a random walk.

The partial autocorrelation at lag kkk is the correlation remaining after removing the effect of the intermediate lags — the direct, as opposed to inherited, dependence.

Physics picture

AR = all-pole filter (resonances); MA = all-zero filter (notches); ARMA = a rational transfer function. Fitting an ARMA model to a detector's noise is measuring its response, and the fitted poles are its resonant modes and damping rates.

Order selection

Use AIC or BIC rather than eye, and check that the residuals are white. Adding orders always improves the fit; it rarely improves prediction.

An AR(2) with complex roots is a damped oscillator driven by noise: the spectrum shows a resonance whose width is the damping rate. Quasi-periodic oscillations in X-ray binaries are often modelled this way.

Two cases worth naming

Random walks and periodic signals

Random walk (unit root)

xt=xt−1+εt,Var⁡(xt)=t σ2,S(f)∝f−2.x_t=x_{t-1}+\varepsilon_t,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(x_t)=t\,\sigma^2,\qquad S(f)\propto f^{-2}.xt​=xt−1​+εt​,Var(xt​)=tσ2,S(f)∝f−2.

Not stationary: the variance grows without limit and there is no mean to return to. Differencing gives white noise. Two independent random walks can look strikingly correlated — the classic spurious regression — so always test for a unit root before claiming a relationship between two drifting series.

Periodic plus noise

xt=Acos⁡(2πf0t+ϕ)+ηt.x_t=A\cos(2\pi f_0 t+\phi)+\eta_t .xt​=Acos(2πf0​t+ϕ)+ηt​.

The autocorrelation oscillates without decaying; the spectrum has a line at f0f_0f0​ whose width is set by the record length, Δf≈1/T\Delta f\approx1/TΔf≈1/T, not by the signal.

Physics picture

The random walk is Brownian motion: the diffusive t\sqrt tt​ growth and the f−2f^{-2}f−2 spectrum are the same statement. In an instrument it is the signature of an unconstrained integrator — a drifting oscillator phase, a thermal drift, an uncontrolled bias.

Detecting a line honestly
  • The height of the tallest peak in NNN independent frequency bins is not a 3σ3\sigma3σ event; account for the number of trials (the "look-elsewhere effect").
  • Assess it against the measured background spectrum, which is usually red, not against white noise.
  • Check that the phase is coherent across the record if the signal should be coherent.

The distinction between a trend and a random walk is genuinely hard and matters: a linear trend extrapolates, a random walk does not.

Beyond the linear stationary family

Four families you will meet in real data

Long memory: 1/fα1/f^\alpha1/fα

Correlations decaying as a power law, not an exponential, so no single correlation time exists. It appears in electronics, in seismology, in pulsar timing and in climate. Modelled as fractional noise, as a sum of many relaxation times, or simply as a power-law spectrum. Beware: it makes long records look as if they contain trends, because they do, of a stochastic kind.

Changing variance (ARCH/GARCH)

Quiet stretches and violent stretches: the mean is unremarkable but the variance itself is a process. Common in turbulence, in market data and in any detector with intermittent interference. Standard tests assume constant variance, and quietly fail here.

State-space models

zt=Fzt−1+wt,yt=Hzt+vt.\boldsymbol z_t=F\boldsymbol z_{t-1}+\boldsymbol w_t,\qquad y_t=H\boldsymbol z_t+v_t .zt​=Fzt−1​+wt​,yt​=Hzt​+vt​.

A hidden state with its own dynamics, observed through noisy measurements. The Kalman filter propagates its mean and covariance exactly for linear Gaussian systems. This is the language of orbit determination, navigation and data assimilation.

Gaussian processes

Give up on a recursion and specify the covariance directly, Cov⁡[x(t),x(t′)]=k(t−t′)\operatorname{Cov}[x(t),x(t')]=k(t-t')Cov[x(t),x(t′)]=k(t−t′). Then prediction, interpolation and gaps are all handled by linear algebra, and irregular sampling causes no trouble. The cost is O(N3)\mathcal O(N^3)O(N3) unless the kernel has special structure (and several useful ones do).

These four are not exclusive: a GP with a Matérn kernel is a continuous AR process, and a state-space model can be written as a GP. The choice is about which description makes your physics easiest to express.
